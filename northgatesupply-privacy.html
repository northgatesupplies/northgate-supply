Privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026.

This policy covers Northgate Supply's website, our shops, our email list and the publishing tool we use with Google, YouTube and Pinterest ("the Northgate app").

The Northgate app and Google user data

The Northgate app is our own internal tool. It signs in only to Northgate Supply's own Google account, to upload and manage videos on our own YouTube channel and to create files in our own Google Drive. It is not offered to the public, and it does not ask anyone else to sign in.

  • What it accesses: through the YouTube Data API, our channel's details and the videos we upload; through the Google Drive API, only files the app itself creates (the drive.file permission).
  • How it is used: only to publish and manage our own videos and files.
  • Storage: sign-in tokens are kept on our own computer, never on a server, and are not shared.
  • Sharing: we do not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone, and do not use it for advertising.
  • Limited Use: Northgate's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • YouTube: the app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Removing access: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. When it is, we delete the stored tokens within 7 days.

The Northgate app and Pinterest data

The same internal tool also connects to Northgate Supply's own Pinterest business account, and to no one else's, through the Pinterest API.

  • What it accesses: our own account's boards and pins, and our account's basic details, so it can make boards and pins for our own products.
  • How it is used: only to publish pins that link to our own shop. A person at Northgate approves each pin before it is published, and pins go out at least three hours apart.
  • Storage and sharing: the sign-in token is kept on our own computer, never on a server. We do not sell, share or transfer Pinterest data, and we do not use it for advertising or to build profiles of anyone.
  • Other people: the tool is not offered to the public and never asks anyone else to sign in, so it holds no other person's Pinterest data.
  • Removing access: access can be revoked at any time in Pinterest under Settings, Apps. When it is, we delete the stored token within 7 days.

Customers

When you buy from us, the purchase is handled by the shop you bought on (Gumroad, Etsy or Amazon) under its own privacy policy. We receive what that shop shares with sellers to deliver your order and answer you, such as your name, email address and what you bought, and we use it only for that. We do not sell it.

Email list

If you join our email list, your email address is held by our email provider (Kit) to send you our emails. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no tracking of its own. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs.

Your choices

Write to us to ask what we hold about you, to correct it or to have it deleted: northgatesupplies@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

Changes
